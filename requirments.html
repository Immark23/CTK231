Program Requirements

Creative Technologies offers several areas of study for students.

Students in the Creative Technologies program can explore different areas depending on their interests and career goals. The program combines creative skills with technology and gives students opportunities to work with digital media, audio, video, gaming, programming, and interactive technologies.

Areas of Study

  • Audio and Music Production
  • Game Design
  • Interdisciplinary Technologies
  • Video
  • Motion Graphics
  • Interactive Technologies

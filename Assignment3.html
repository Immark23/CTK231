Creative Technologies

The School of Creative Technologies at Illinois State University offers students opportunities to study technology, design, media, audio, video, gaming, and other creative fields.

Students can explore areas such as audio and music production, game design, motion graphics, and interactive technologies.

About the Program

Creative Technologies combines creativity and technology to prepare students for careers in the digital media industry.
